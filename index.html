Before you drop in

Good to know.

How much will my detail cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle’s size, condition and the work you need. Send a quote enquiry or call the team to discuss it before booking.

Do you clean larger vehicles?

Big K welcomes enquiries for cars, 4WDs, trucks, buses and work vehicles. For larger vehicles or multiple vehicles, call ahead with their details so the team can confirm suitability and availability.

Should I book before bringing my vehicle?

Please call first to discuss the service, availability and how long the work may take. A quote enquiry doesn’t reserve a booking until the team confirms it with you.
